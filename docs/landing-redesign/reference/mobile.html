Connecting is optional

Nothing is requested from Google until you connect an account, and you can disconnect at any time from your settings, which revokes the access immediately.

Bookings go out; busy times come back

Haab writes one event per confirmed booking and reads busy times from the calendars you nominate, so a commitment already on your calendar blocks that slot.

Your clients’ details never leave Haab

Events carry the service name and nothing else. A client’s name, email, phone, and notes are never written to Google, because a calendar can be shared and Haab does not decide who may read them.

TRUST & PRIVACY

Built to be trusted with a real calendar.

Especially when the calendar belongs to a clinic, and the people booking are patients.

The server decides, not the browser

A held slot is held on the server. When two people reach for the same time, one is confirmed and the other is told immediately — the page cannot talk itself into a double booking.

No accounts means no passwords to lose

Your clients never register, so there is no client password to store, leak, or reset. Their booking is reached through a private link that is stored hashed, never in plain text.

Your page is yours to publish

Nothing is public until you publish it, and you can take it down again from your dashboard without deleting your work.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What works today?

Public booking pages, live availability, ten-minute holds, confirmations, event capacity, and a private self-service link on every booking.

Can I take registrations for a class or event?

Yes. Events count confirmed and held spots against your capacity, so a class closes itself when it fills.

Full-day bookings, not just appointments?

Yes. Appointments, full days, and registrations share one page.

Can bookings be added to a calendar?

Yes. Every confirmation includes a calendar file. It also includes a private QR for opening or verifying the booking.
