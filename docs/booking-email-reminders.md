# Booking email reminders

Haab emails clients before confirmed appointments. Reminders are on by default for new businesses, with the first email set for 24 hours before the appointment. You can add a second reminder, set different lead times, or turn reminders off.

In your dashboard, open **Settings → Email reminders** to choose the timing and add an optional short message. You can also turn reminders off for an individual service in that service's editor. Times follow your business timezone.

Each reminder uses the client's booking language (English or Spanish) and includes the service, date, time, duration, business name, and private link to reschedule or cancel. Clients need no account. Client notes are never included.

Cancelling a booking stops pending reminders. Rescheduling replaces them with reminders for the new time. If a client books after a reminder's lead time has passed, Haab does not send that reminder late. Delivery depends on your booking email sender and the scheduled worker being configured; see [operations](operations/email-reminders.md) for setup and troubleshooting.
